# Security policy

GulfVestor Properties takes the security and privacy of its systems seriously.

## Reporting a concern

Please do not open a public GitHub issue for a suspected vulnerability. Email [info@gulfvestorproperties.com](mailto:info@gulfvestorproperties.com) with:

- the affected URL or service;
- clear reproduction steps;
- the potential impact; and
- a safe way to contact you.

Do not include live credentials, unrelated personal information, or data obtained from systems or records outside the minimum needed to demonstrate the concern. Do not disrupt service or attempt to access another person's account or information.

Additional guidance is available on our [Trust & Security page](https://gulfvestorproperties.com/trust-security.html).
